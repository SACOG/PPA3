Active Transportation Program

Program objectives and how they integrate with the PPA Report

Focus Area: The Active Transportation Program (ATP) is a statewide funding program meant to increase walking and bicycling by funding projects that provide safe and connected infrastructure.

Key Performance Objectives: Providing bicycle and pedestrian infrastructure, education, plans, and trails to improve connections and increase riding, walking, and rolling.
